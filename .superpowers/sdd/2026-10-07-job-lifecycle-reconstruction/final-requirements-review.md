## Important findings — one complete correction list

### F1 — Archived version retirement cannot restore ordinary version admission

**Locations:** `job_discovery/lifecycle/identity.py:264`, `:326`, `:355`, `:462`; `job_discovery/lifecycle/maintenance.py:180`. **Binding:** design §7, lines 435–438 and 450–452; Task 7's explicitly deferred archive/reference-aware retirement prerequisite.

`_version_room` refuses a changed version whenever any existing version, including the current one, is older than 30 days, or the total is already 11. Maintenance excludes the current version unconditionally. Consequently a listing with one 31-day-old current version, **even with exact acknowledged version/hash coverage and no cache/private/edge reference**, cannot admit its next meaningful change and cannot retire the blocking row. Repeated sweeps do not change that state. Admission leaves Job metadata unchanged; a changed demanded JD can terminal-defer at `capture_version` despite successful fetch.

There is also a count-bound mismatch: at the admitted maximum of current + ten superseded, the oldest superseded row has only nine newer superseded rows. `_version_batch` requires at least ten. Thus count pressure alone can never open a slot through the normal admitted state. Finally, each known location creates a `job_locations` FK to its version, while maintenance excludes every such reference and has no superseded public-edge retirement phase/caller. Those ordinary public edges can keep otherwise archived versions permanently ineligible.

The existing pause is correct when an event is unarchived or a real active/private reference still requires the row. That is **not** the finding. The defect is the absence of a reachable bounded progression for the ordinary fully archived, otherwise disposable case after all 13 tasks. Task 7's interim fail-closed behavior was explicitly waiting for this later integration; it cannot be represented as completed retirement now.

**Correction:** provide an exact-archive-proof-aware, bounded version/edge retirement and replacement path that can make room at the prospective count/age boundary, while retaining current facts, genuinely needed references, immutable private inputs, pending events and exact coverage. Preserve public archive semantics when retiring local edges; local compaction must not falsely assert that a public relation disappeared. Do not merely drop the age/count check or weaken archive/reference protection.

**Ordinary evidence:** actual admission → exact archive acknowledgement → maintenance/replacement → changed admission for (a) an old current with no other references, (b) current + ten superseded, and (c) an archived superseded known-location edge; show bounded commits and unchanged protected/unarchived cases. Current admission tests establish pausing, not resumption. The old `test_only_safely_archived_unreferenced_superseded_versions_retire` manually inserts 15 versions and supplies a listing watermark without the final exact-coverage rows; it predates the final contract and is not selected by final CI. Update that ordinary fixture rather than citing its earlier pass as final proof.

### F2 — A completed detail demand permanently protects disposable payload and never ages out

**Locations:** `job_discovery/lifecycle/maintenance.py:25` and `:274`; `job_discovery/lifecycle/demand.py:110`, `:134`; `dashboard/lib/jobLifecycle.ts:139`. **Binding:** design §4 lines 260–276 and §6 lines 386–400.

`_UNPROTECTED` excludes a Job whenever **any** demand has a non-NULL description or question snapshot, regardless of terminal age, actual consumption or persisted approval/correction/application work. Every successful `_finish(..., 'ready')` creates precisely such a snapshot. Terminal cleanup only selects demands with **both** snapshot columns NULL. There is no caller that retires these disposable terminal captures.

A user can simply open a detail, complete hydration and leave without creating protected work. That ready demand then protects the shared cache indefinitely and itself retains the full captured body indefinitely. Once the ready-reuse TTL elapses, a later explicit request can create another full capture while the old one remains. This defeats both bounded terminal-demand retention and the intended unprotected 30-day/7-day cache policy. It is independent of the legitimate requirement to preserve a package's or review's immutable owner input.

**Correction:** distinguish temporary demand captures/receipts and their actual-use/cache lifetime from durable protected owner work. Implement bounded terminal retirement/compaction and shared-cache eligibility under the specified policy, keeping needed active-generation inputs, actual private snapshots, exact consumption identity and compact replay state. A live/needed input must not disappear midway through its consumer. Where an old origin receipt is legitimately gone, use the already accepted genuine new private-copy contract; do not invent historical lineage. Coordinate with F6's producer-completion gap.

**Ordinary evidence:** actual detail-only hydration with no saved work, normal terminal aging and cache TTL, repeated explicit demand, plus a saved package/review and active consumer whose exact inputs remain intact. The six-family E2E only checks that a newly completed demand protects one cache during that sweep; it does not demonstrate eventual retirement. No cross-user or old enforcement probes are requested.
